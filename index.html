Why Back on Track

Built for people who
already use a spreadsheet

📊
Your data stays yours

No new database. No subscription. Your Google Sheet is the source of truth — the app just makes it beautiful on your phone.

⚡
Real-time sync

Add a transaction in the app and it appears in your sheet instantly. Edit your sheet on desktop and pull it back with one tap.

🎯
Intelligent on-track signal

A blended 7-day and monthly average tells you instantly whether you're on pace — and exactly how many low-spend days it takes to recover.

📈
Live forecast

The chart shows actual spend to today, then projects forward. Touch and hold any point to see exact figures for that day.

📱
Installs like a real app

Add to your iPhone home screen from Safari. No App Store. No updates to manage. Works offline after the first load.

🔧
Fits your sheet structure

Pick which tab and columns hold your data. Date in B, amount in C, category in E? No problem — any layout works.
